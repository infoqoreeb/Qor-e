Featured Results

Real Campaigns, Real Outcomes

Debut Romance Author: From Zero to 10,000 Readers

The Challenge: A first-time romance author with no existing platform, no email list, and no social media following was preparing to self-publish her debut novel. She had a limited budget and a three-month timeline before her planned publication date.

The Strategy: We built a complete launch infrastructure from scratch. This included creating a lead magnet (a bonus epilogue novella), building a landing page, setting up a MailerLite email system, developing a pre-launch social media strategy focused on BookTok and Bookstagram, and creating a multi-platform advertising campaign targeting readers of comparable romance titles.

The Results: By launch day, the author had an email list of 2,400 subscribers and a combined social media following of 6,800. Launch week generated over 1,200 sales, pushing the book into the top 50 of its Amazon subcategory. Within 90 days post-launch, the book had accumulated over 300 verified reviews and sustained an average of 40 to 60 sales per day through organic Amazon visibility.

Midlist Thriller Author: Breaking Through the Plateau

The Challenge: A traditionally published thriller author with four previous books was consistently selling 200 to 400 copies per title. His publisher was providing minimal marketing support, and his sales had plateaued despite strong reviews and a loyal core readership.

The Strategy: We conducted a comprehensive audit of his backlist performance and identified significant untapped potential in his Amazon keyword and category positioning. We restructured his Amazon Ads campaigns, developed a new book description using conversion copywriting principles, optimized his Author Central page, and built an email capture system that converted his existing readers into subscribers.

The Results: Within 60 days of implementing the new strategy, his backlist sales increased by 180 percent. His fifth book, launched with our full launch architecture service, sold 3,500 copies in its first month, a 700 percent improvement over his previous launches. His email list grew from zero to 5,200 subscribers within six months.

Nonfiction Business Author: Corporate Sales Breakthrough

The Challenge: A business leadership author had published a well-reviewed book that was selling modestly on Amazon but was not reaching the corporate buyers and organizational decision-makers who represented the highest-value market for his content.

The Strategy: We shifted his marketing focus from consumer retail to B2B channels. This included developing a corporate outreach email campaign, creating a bulk order landing page, optimizing his LinkedIn presence with thought leadership content, and developing a speaking kit that positioned the book as a natural complement to corporate training programs.

The Results: Within four months, the author secured three corporate bulk orders totaling 2,800 copies. His LinkedIn following grew by 4,000 connections, and he received invitations to speak at two industry conferences. His Amazon sales also increased by 65 percent as the corporate activity boosted his book's visibility and credibility.

Fantasy Series Author: Long-Term Growth System

The Challenge: A self-published epic fantasy author with a three-book series was seeing strong sales for book one but experiencing significant drop-off for books two and three. She needed a strategy to improve series read-through and build a sustainable long-term career.

The Strategy: We implemented a series funnel optimization strategy that included rewriting the book one description to emphasize the series arc, adding series-linked advertising campaigns on Amazon, building an email nurture sequence that introduced book one readers to the subsequent titles, and creating a reader magnet that bridged the gap between books.

The Results: Series read-through from book one to book two increased from 35 percent to 62 percent. Read-through from book two to book three increased from 28 percent to 55 percent. Overall series revenue increased by 210 percent within six months, and the author's email list grew to 8,500 subscribers who were actively engaged with the series.
