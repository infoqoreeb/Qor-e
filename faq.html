FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

We have compiled answers to the most common questions authors ask before working with us. If your question is not covered here, email us at info.qoreb@gmail.com and we will provide a detailed response within 24 to 48 hours.

What types of authors do you work with?

We work with fiction and nonfiction authors across every genre, including romance, thriller, science fiction, fantasy, horror, mystery, literary fiction, historical fiction, young adult, middle grade, business, self-help, memoir, health and wellness, Christian living, and poetry. We work with first-time debut authors, midlist authors looking to break through, and established authors who want to scale their results. We work with traditionally published authors whose publishers are not providing adequate marketing support, and we work with self-published authors who are building their careers independently. The common thread among all of our clients is a serious commitment to their writing career and a willingness to invest in professional marketing.

How much does your service cost?

Our pricing varies based on the specific services you need, the scope of your campaign, your genre, and your goals. We do not offer one-size-fits-all packages because every author's situation is different. A debut author launching their first novel has very different needs and budget constraints than an established author with a backlist of ten titles. When you email us at info.qoreb@gmail.com with details about your book and your goals, we will provide a customized proposal with transparent pricing that reflects exactly what you need and nothing you do not. We believe in honest pricing with no hidden fees, no surprise charges, and no long-term contracts that lock you into services you no longer need.

Why do you only communicate through email?

We conduct all communication exclusively through Gmail at info.qoreb@gmail.com for several important reasons. First, email creates a permanent written record of every conversation, decision, and strategic recommendation. You never have to rely on memory or worry about miscommunication because the complete history is always available in your inbox. Second, email allows us to provide more thorough and thoughtful responses than a rushed conversation would permit. Complex marketing strategies require detailed explanations, and email gives us the space to provide them. Third, email respects your time and ours. You can read and respond to our messages on your own schedule, whether that is early in the morning, late at night, or during a break in your writing routine. There is no need to coordinate availability or block out time for appointments. We have found that this system produces better outcomes for our clients because it eliminates miscommunication and ensures that every strategic decision is fully documented.

How quickly will I see results?

The timeline for results depends on the specific services you engage and your starting position. Some tactics produce immediate results: a well-optimized Amazon Ads campaign can start generating sales within the first week, and a rewritten book description can improve your conversion rate almost immediately after it goes live. Other tactics take longer to build momentum: email list growth, social media audience building, and organic search visibility typically require several months of consistent effort before the results become significant. Book launch campaigns are designed to produce concentrated results during a specific window, while long-term growth strategies are designed to compound over time. During our initial consultation, we will provide a realistic timeline for your specific situation so you know exactly what to expect and when to expect it. We will never promise overnight success or guarantee specific sales numbers, because anyone who makes those promises is not being honest with you.

Do you guarantee bestseller status or specific sales numbers?

No, and you should be deeply skeptical of any book marketing service that does. Bestseller status depends on a complex combination of factors including genre, timing, competition, market conditions, book quality, cover design, pricing, and reader reception, many of which are outside the control of any marketing service. What we do guarantee is that we will deploy proven strategies, execute campaigns with precision, provide transparent reporting, and make data-driven adjustments to maximize your results. We guarantee professionalism, expertise, and honest communication. We guarantee that every dollar of your budget will be spent strategically and accounted for. But we will never guarantee a specific sales number or a bestseller list placement, because doing so would be dishonest.

Do I need to have a finished book before I contact you?

No. In fact, the earlier you begin planning your marketing, the more effective your launch will be. We regularly work with authors who are still in the writing or editing phase of their book. Starting early gives us time to build your email list, develop your author platform, research your competitive landscape, and prepare your launch infrastructure so that everything is ready to go the moment your book is published. Ideally, we recommend reaching out at least three to six months before your planned publication date, but we have also worked with authors who contacted us just weeks before launch and still achieved strong results. Whenever you are in your journey, email us at info.qoreb@gmail.com and we will let you know how we can help at your current stage.

Can you help with a book that has already been published?

Absolutely. Many of our clients come to us with books that have been published for months or even years but have never received the marketing support they deserve. A book's publication date is not a deadline for marketing; it is a starting point. We have helped authors dramatically increase sales for books that were published years ago by implementing new advertising strategies, rewriting book descriptions, optimizing Amazon keywords and categories, building email lists, and re-launching titles with fresh campaigns. If your book is still available for sale, it is never too late to invest in marketing.

What is the minimum budget I need for advertising?

The minimum effective advertising budget varies by genre, platform, and goals, but we generally recommend a minimum of $300 to $500 per month for paid advertising to generate meaningful data and results. With budgets below that threshold, it becomes difficult to gather enough performance data to optimize campaigns effectively. That said, we understand that not every author has a large marketing budget, and we are happy to work within your means. For authors with limited budgets, we often recommend focusing on organic strategies first (email list building, social media content, Goodreads optimization, and book club outreach) and adding paid advertising when the budget allows. We will always be honest about what is realistic given your financial situation.

Do you work with traditionally published authors?

Yes. Many traditionally published authors come to us because their publishers are providing minimal marketing support, which is an increasingly common situation in the publishing industry. Even authors at major publishing houses often find that their publisher's marketing efforts are limited to the first few weeks after publication and that the bulk of the long-term marketing responsibility falls on the author. We work with traditionally published authors to supplement and extend their publisher's efforts, building independent marketing infrastructure that gives them more control over their career trajectory and more visibility in the marketplace.

What makes Qore'eb different from other book marketing services?

Three things set us apart. First, we are exclusively focused on books. We do not market restaurants, software companies, e-commerce stores, or any other type of business. Every strategy we develop, every campaign we run, and every system we build is designed specifically for the book market. Second, we provide the complete spectrum of book marketing services under one roof, which means your campaigns are integrated and cohesive rather than fragmented across multiple providers. Third, our email-only communication model ensures complete transparency and documentation of every interaction, which eliminates miscommunication and keeps both parties accountable. We also pride ourselves on honesty: we will tell you what we think will work, what we think will not work, and what the realistic outcomes are for your specific situation, even if that means telling you that our services are not the right fit for your current needs.

How do I get started?

Getting started is simple. Send an email to info.qoreb@gmail.com with the following information: your name, your book's title and genre, your publication status (published, upcoming, or in progress), your primary marketing goals, your approximate budget range, and any specific challenges you are currently facing. We will respond within 24 to 48 hours with an honest assessment of your situation and a clear outline of how we can help. If we are a good fit, we will send you a detailed proposal with specific recommendations, timelines, and pricing. There is no pressure, no obligation, and no high-pressure sales tactics. We believe that the best client relationships are built on mutual trust and clear expectations, and that starts with an honest first conversation.

Still Have Questions?

We are happy to answer any question you have about book marketing, our services, or your specific situation. Email us at info.qoreb@gmail.com and we will get back to you with a detailed, thoughtful response.
