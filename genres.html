Genre Expertise

Marketing Strategies for Every Genre

Book marketing is not one-size-fits-all. The strategies that drive massive sales for a steamy romance novel will fall flat for a literary memoir, and the tactics that work for a business leadership book will not resonate with young adult fantasy readers. At Qore'eb, we develop genre-specific marketing strategies that account for the unique reader behaviors, competitive dynamics, promotional channels, and market conventions of every category.

Fiction Genres

Stories That Sell

Romance

The romance genre is the highest-selling fiction category in the world, and it is also one of the most competitive. Romance readers are voracious, loyal, and highly engaged on social media platforms like Instagram and TikTok (BookTok). Our romance marketing strategies leverage tropes, heat levels, and subgenre specificity to target the exact reader segments most likely to devour your books. We focus on series read-through optimization, newsletter swaps with complementary romance authors, and social media content that taps into the emotional connections romance readers crave.

Thriller and Suspense

Thriller readers are driven by tension, pacing, and the promise of a twist they will not see coming. Our thriller marketing strategies emphasize hook-driven ad copy, suspenseful book trailers, and targeted Amazon Ads campaigns that position your book alongside the genre's biggest names. We also leverage BookBub promotions and Kindle Unlimited enrollment strategies that are particularly effective in this category.

Science Fiction and Fantasy

SFF readers are among the most passionate and community-oriented readers in publishing. They congregate on Reddit, Goodreads, and dedicated forums where word-of-mouth recommendations carry enormous weight. Our SFF marketing strategies focus on community engagement, world-building content, series launch sequencing, and targeted advertising on platforms where genre fans actively discuss and discover new titles.

Horror

Horror is a genre with a fiercely dedicated fanbase that values atmosphere, dread, and originality. Our horror marketing strategies emphasize mood-driven visual content, strategic timing around seasonal peaks (particularly the fall and Halloween season), and targeted outreach to horror-specific review blogs, podcasts, and reading communities.

Mystery and Crime

Mystery readers are loyal series followers who value intricate plotting and satisfying resolutions. Our mystery marketing strategies focus on series funnel optimization, cozy mystery community engagement, and targeted advertising that highlights the specific subgenre elements (cozy, police procedural, psychological, etc.) that your target readers are searching for.

Literary and Historical Fiction

Literary and historical fiction readers are drawn to beautiful prose, complex characters, and immersive settings. Our strategies for these genres emphasize review generation from literary publications, book club outreach, award submission guidance, and content marketing that highlights the craft and research behind your work.

Nonfiction Genres

Ideas That Spread

Business and Leadership

Business book marketing requires positioning you as a thought leader, not just an author. Our strategies include LinkedIn content campaigns, podcast outreach, speaking opportunity identification, and corporate bulk sales strategies that can generate significant revenue beyond individual retail sales.

Self-Help and Personal Development

Self-help readers are actively searching for solutions to specific problems. Our strategies focus on keyword-optimized Amazon listings, content marketing that demonstrates your expertise, email funnels that deliver genuine value, and strategic partnerships with influencers and platforms in the personal development space.

Memoir and Biography

Memoir marketing is deeply personal and requires a delicate balance between authenticity and strategic positioning. We help you identify the universal themes in your personal story that will resonate with the widest possible audience, then craft campaigns that connect your experience to the experiences of your target readers.

Health, Wellness, and Christian Living

These categories require trust-building marketing that establishes your credibility and authority. Our strategies include content-driven email sequences, community engagement in faith-based and wellness-focused groups, and targeted advertising that reaches readers who are actively seeking guidance in these areas.

Young Readers

Young Adult and Middle Grade

Marketing books for younger readers requires understanding that the purchasing decision-maker (often a parent, librarian, or teacher) is frequently different from the end reader (the young person themselves). Our YA and middle grade strategies address both audiences simultaneously, leveraging BookTok and Bookstagram trends for teen readers while also targeting the educators and parents who influence purchasing decisions. We also focus on school and library outreach, educator resource development, and age-appropriate social media strategies.
