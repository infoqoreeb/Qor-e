Service

Brand Systems and Platform Optimization

Your author brand is the sum total of every impression a reader has of you across every platform, every piece of content, and every interaction. A strong, consistent author brand builds trust, creates recognition, and makes every marketing dollar you spend more effective. A weak or inconsistent brand does the opposite: it confuses readers, dilutes your message, and wastes opportunities.

What We Do

Building a Cohesive Author Brand From the Ground Up

Our Brand Systems service begins with a comprehensive audit of your current author presence across all platforms. We examine your Amazon Author Central page, your Goodreads profile, your website, your social media accounts, your email communications, your book covers, and your promotional materials. We identify every inconsistency, every missed opportunity, and every element that is not working as hard as it should be.

Based on that audit, we develop a complete brand system that includes your visual identity guidelines (color palette, typography, imagery style, and logo usage), your messaging framework (tagline, positioning statement, value proposition, and tone of voice), and your platform optimization plan (specific recommendations for improving every author-facing platform where readers encounter your work).

Amazon Author Central optimization is a critical component that many authors overlook. Your Author Central page is often the first place readers go after discovering your book, and it needs to reinforce the impression your cover and description created. We optimize your biography, your photos, your blog feed, your video content, and your book catalog to create a compelling author profile that converts browsers into buyers.

Platform Focus

Every Touchpoint, Fully Optimized

Amazon Author Central

Complete optimization of your Author Central profile including biography, photos, videos, blog integration, and book catalog organization. We ensure your page ranks well in Amazon search and presents a professional, compelling image to potential readers.

Goodreads Profile

Your Goodreads author profile is your home base on the world's largest book discovery platform. We optimize your profile information, author photo, blog feed, and book listings to maximize your visibility and credibility among Goodreads' 90 million members.

Website and Landing Pages

Your author website is the one piece of digital real estate you fully control. We audit your site for conversion optimization, mobile responsiveness, loading speed, and brand consistency, then provide specific recommendations for improvement.

Cross-Platform Consistency

Readers should recognize your brand instantly whether they encounter you on Amazon, Instagram, Goodreads, or your email newsletter. We develop brand guidelines that ensure visual and messaging consistency across every platform and touchpoint.
