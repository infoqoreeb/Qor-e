Service

Email Infrastructure and Automation

Your email list is the single most valuable marketing asset you will ever own as an author. Unlike social media followers, who are subject to algorithm changes and platform policies beyond your control, your email subscribers are people who have explicitly given you permission to communicate with them directly. We build the complete email infrastructure that turns casual readers into loyal, repeat buyers.

The Full System

From Lead Magnet to Lifetime Reader

Most authors who have email lists treat them as simple broadcast channels: they send an email when they have a new book out, and that is about it. This approach leaves enormous revenue and engagement on the table. A properly built email system works 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, automatically nurturing new subscribers, re-engaging dormant readers, and driving consistent book sales without requiring your constant attention.

We build complete email ecosystems that include every component of a high-performing author email system. This starts with lead magnet development: we help you create a compelling free offering (such as a bonus short story, a deleted chapter, a prequel novella, a character guide, or a curated resource list) that gives readers a powerful incentive to join your list. We then build the landing page that converts visitors into subscribers, the welcome sequence that introduces new readers to your world and your books, the nurture sequences that maintain engagement between book releases, and the launch sequences that drive concentrated sales during your publication windows.

We work with all major email service providers including MailerLite, ConvertKit, Mailchimp, and ActiveCampaign. If you already have an email platform, we will build within it. If you do not, we will recommend the best option for your specific needs and budget and handle the complete setup.

Components

What Your Email Infrastructure Includes

Lead Magnet Strategy and Creation

We identify the highest-converting lead magnet format for your genre, develop the content, and design the delivery mechanism. Your lead magnet needs to attract the right readers, not just any readers, and we ensure it aligns perfectly with your book's audience.

Welcome Sequence Architecture

Your welcome sequence is the most important series of emails you will ever send. These are the first five to seven emails a new subscriber receives, and they determine whether that person becomes an engaged reader or an inactive address on your list. We craft sequences that build connection, deliver value, and introduce your books naturally.

Automated Nurture Campaigns

We build evergreen email sequences that run automatically in the background, keeping your readers engaged between book releases. These sequences include reading recommendations, behind-the-scenes content, exclusive previews, and strategic soft-sell emails that maintain your visibility without feeling pushy.

Launch and Sales Sequences

When it is time to sell, your email list should be your most powerful weapon. We build launch sequences that create anticipation, drive urgency, and generate concentrated sales bursts that trigger Amazon's algorithmic recommendations and push your book up the charts.
